# Data Notice

The prototype seed data uses publicly visible World of Sea Battle community/reference pages as planning sources.

- Ship names/rates/classes and crew-oriented context: World of Sea Battle Wiki – Ships
- Items, weapons, ammunition, consumables, materials and trade goods: World of Sea Battle Wiki – Items / Weapons
- Slot model context: official Update B17 notes describing sail and upgrade slots
- Some upgrade-name seeds are treated as editable planner/meta rows because public exact upgrade data is incomplete and may change by patch.

No game images or copyrighted item icons are bundled. The frontend uses a local placeholder SVG for future icon replacement.
