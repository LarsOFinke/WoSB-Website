# Notices and asset boundary

The original project code, documentation, and neutral redraws are licensed
under [AGPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE.md).

Game-derived material is **not** covered by that license. In particular, the
in-game screenshots under `docs/ingame-screenshots/` and the derived crops
under `frontend/game-assets/` are retained as inactive reference material
while permission from the relevant rights holder is pending. They must not be
published, redistributed, or enabled in a hosted build. See
[asset licensing](docs/ASSET_LICENSING.md).

The prototype seed data uses publicly visible World of Sea Battle community/reference pages as planning sources.

- Ship names/rates/classes and crew-oriented context: World of Sea Battle Wiki – Ships
- Items, weapons, ammunition, consumables, materials and trade goods: World of Sea Battle Wiki – Items / Weapons
- Slot model context: official Update B17 notes describing sail and upgrade slots
- Some upgrade-name seeds are treated as editable planner/meta rows because public exact upgrade data is incomplete and may change by patch.

Public builds use neutral, repository-owned placeholder/redraw assets by
default. The frontend's explicit game mode remains authorization-gated and
is not approved for public deployment.
